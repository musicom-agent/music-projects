Method 034: PCFG Recursion

Probabilistic Context-Free Grammar models musical form as a formal syntax tree. It recursively resolves song-level symbols through phrase-level, motif-level, and note-level production rules with weighted probabilities. This maps Chomskyan structures to a perfectly-aligned 16-step grid of sixteenth notes per bar.

Chomskyan Rewrite System:
S → Bar_A Bar_B Bar_A Bar_C Bar_B Bar_A Bar_C Bar_D [p=1.0]
Bar_A → Phrase_1 Phrase_1 [p=0.7] | Phrase_1 Phrase_2 [p=0.3]
Phrase_1 → Motif_A Motif_B [p=0.7] | Motif_A Motif_A [p=0.3]
Motif_A → n0 n2 n3 n4 [p=0.5] | n0 n3 n2 n3 [p=0.5]

Method SP-021: Binaural Spatialization

Woodworth-Schlosberg ITD & Dynamic ILD models realistic 3D acoustic placement. Applies sub-millisecond fractional delay propagation times to far ears and frequency-dependent digital lowpass diffraction filters to emulate head-shadowing.

Interaural Time Difference (ITD):
τ(θ) = (a / c) · (sin|θ| + |θ|)
τ_far = τ(θ), τ_near = 0

Interaural Level Difference (ILD):
f_c(θ) = f_min + (f_max - f_min) · [(1 + cosθ) / 2]^p
y[n] = α_f · x[n] + (1 - α_f) · y[n-1]
