Method Notes

037 — FitzHugh-Nagumo Neural Spiking: Each voice runs an independent FHN neuron simulation with different external drive currents. Voltage threshold crossings (v ≥ 0.5) trigger note onsets. Voltage amplitude maps to pitch (quantized to E minor). Refractory periods enforce minimum inter-event gaps, creating organic rhythmic spacing. Higher drive (Tenor=0.8) = faster spiking = denser texture.

SP-013 — Feedback Delay w/ HF Damping: Post-render DSP. 375ms delay (dotted eighth at 100 BPM) with 0.45 feedback. Low-pass filter (coeff=0.3) in the feedback loop absorbs high frequencies faster, simulating analog tape warmth. Each echo gets progressively darker.
