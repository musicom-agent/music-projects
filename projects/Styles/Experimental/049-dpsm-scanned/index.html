Method 026: DPSM Phasing

Deconstructive Phase-Shift Minimalism maps identical, loop-based musical layers onto a structural timeline while introducing fractional step offsets to secondary layers. Over successive sections, the secondary voice slides out of phase, creating highly syncopated emergent harmonies and polyrhythmic interference patterns.

Phase-Offset Modulo Mapping:
P_v[t] = LoopPitches[(t + φ_{v, s}) mod L]
R_v[t] = LoopTriggers[(t + φ_{v, s}) mod L]
Where φ_{1, s} = 0 (static) and φ_{2, s} = s - 1 (incrementing offset)

Method SP-018: Scanned Synthesis

Scanned Synthesis is a physical-modeling synthesis method. A 1D mass-spring-damper closed string is solved at a slow control rate (100Hz), updating mass positions and velocities. An audio cursor scans this shape at the pitch's fundamental frequency, creating a dynamically evolving wavetable.

Spring-Mass System ODE Update:
a_i[k] = k_s(x_{i+1} - 2x_i + x_{i-1}) - k_c x_i - d_s v_i
v_i[k+1] = v_i[k] + a_i[k] · Δt
x_i[k+1] = x_i[k] + v_i[k+1] · Δt
Scan Phase: θ[n] = (θ[n-1] + f_{fund}/f_s) mod 1
