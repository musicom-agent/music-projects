Method 035: Physarum Foraging (PPTNO)

Physarum Polycephalum Transport Network Optimization models the biological foraging behavior of slime mold. Food sources are placed at specific pitches on a 2D canvas, simulating protoplasmic tube growth, fluid-flow reinforcement, and path pruning to optimize voice-leading and rhythmic density over 4 distinct developmental phases.

Flow Conservation:
∑_j Q_ij(t) = I_i(t)
Q_ij(t) = (D_ij(t) / L_ij) · (p_i(t) - p_j(t))

Conductivity Evolution:
dD_ij/dt = |Q_ij(t)| - r · D_ij(t)

Method SP-024: Bowed String physical Modeling

Friction-Induced Waveguide Synthesis models string-bow dynamics. Bidirectional delay-lines represent neck and bridge wave propagation, while stick-slip rosin friction is solved dynamically at every sample using Newton-Raphson iteration.

Stick-Slip Friction Model:
μ(v_rel) = v_rel · e^(-a · v_rel^2)

System Non-linearity Solver (Newton-Raphson):
H(v_rel) = v_rel - K · v_rel · e^(-a · v_rel^2) - v_input = 0
v_rel_next = v_rel - H(v_rel) / H'(v_rel)
