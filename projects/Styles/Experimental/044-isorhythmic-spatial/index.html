Composition Strategy & Math

This composition applies Isorhythmic Talea-Color Mapping (ITCM) (Method 032) to generate an evolving polyphonic structure, paired with Binaural Woodworth-Schlosberg Spatialization (Method SP-021) to position the lead voice in a dynamic 3D orbit around the listener's head.

1. Isorhythmic Talea-Color Mapping (Method 032)

By decoupling a repeating rhythmic loop (the talea) from a repeating pitch loop (the color) of coprime lengths (&gcd;(T, C) = 1), the pitch-rhythm alignment shifts dynamically over time, creating long-form organic evolutions with zero local redundancy.

  • Lead Voice (Violin/Synth): Talea length = 9, Color length = 7. Coprime cycle = 63 events. Section B retrograde and invert the loops around the central G4 axis.
  • Bass Voice (Bass): Talea length = 5, Color length = 4. Coprime cycle = 20 events.
  • Pad Voice (Synth Pad): Talea length = 3, Color length = 5 chords. Coprime cycle = 15 chords.
  • Drums Voice (Percussion): Talea length = 6, Color length = 5 drums. Coprime cycle = 30 events.

2. Binaural Woodworth-Schlosberg Spatialization (Method SP-021)

The Lead Violin voice is spatialized using the Woodworth-Schlosberg formula, simulating the sub-millisecond Interaural Time Difference (ITD) and the frequency-dependent Interaural Level Difference (ILD) of human head shadowing. The violin dynamically orbits the listener's head in a 3D circle at 0.125 Hz (one rotation every 8 seconds).

ITD Delay: τ(θ) = (a / c) * (sin|θ| + |θ|)
where head radius a = 0.0875 m, speed of sound c = 343 m/s.
ILD Far-Ear Shadowing cutoff f_c(θ) drops from 20 kHz to 1 kHz based on angle θ.
