Method 034: PCFG Recursion

Probabilistic Context-Free Grammar (PCFG) Recursion builds musical structures hierarchically. Symbols representing bars of lead, pads, or bass are recursively expanded down to rhythmic/pitch terminals according to rules weighted by style probabilities, preserving symmetry across voices.

Grammar Transition:
S → LEAD_BAR_I PAD_BAR_I BASS_BAR_I | LEAD_BAR_V PAD_BAR_V BASS_BAR_V …
LEAD_BAR_I → HALF_L_I1 HALF_L_I2 (prob: 0.7)
HALF_L_I1 → QUARTER_L_I1 QUARTER_L_I2 (prob: 0.8) | n0_h (prob: 0.2)
QUARTER_L_I1 → n0_q (prob: 0.5) | n2_e n4_e (prob: 0.5)

Method SP-023: Clarinet Digital Waveguide

Digital Waveguide Woodwind Synthesis models physical acoustics. A bidirectional delay-line tracks wave propagation. A non-linear reed table maps mouth pressure and reflection pressure difference to a reflection coefficient, and an open bell acts as a filter reflection.

Waveguide Delay:
D = fs / (2 · f0)

Reed Interaction:
ΔP = P_mouth - P_reflected
R_reed = slope · ΔP + offset (clamped [-1, 1])
P_forward = P_reflected + ΔP · R_reed
