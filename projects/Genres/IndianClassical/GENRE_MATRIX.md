# Genre Matrix: Indian Classical

## L1 Patterns (Core DNA)

### 1. Raga (Melodic Framework)
- **Concept:** More than a scale; a set of rules for movement (Arohana/Avrohana), characteristic phrases (Pakad), and Vadi/Samvadi (King/Queen notes).
- **Pitch DNA:** Microtonal inflections (Shruti) and slides (Meend).
- **Ahir Bhairav:** C Db E F G A Bb (1 b2 3 4 5 6 b7). Deep, morning mood. Mixing Bhairav (lower tetra) and Kafi (upper tetra).

### 2. Tala (Rhythmic Cycle)
- **Concept:** Cyclic time framework. 
- **Teental:** 16 beats (4+4+4+4). 
- **Bol:** Dha Dhin Dhin Dha | Dha Dhin Dhin Dha | Dha Tin Tin Ta | Ta Dhin Dhin Dha.
- **Vibhag:** 3 claps (Taali) and 1 wave (Khali at beat 9).

### 3. Drone (Tanpura)
- **Concept:** The immutable foundation. Continuous sounding of Tonic (Sa) and often the 5th (Pa) or 4th (Ma).

### 4. Elements
- **Alap:** Improvisation without pulse.
- **Gat:** Fixed composition with rhythmic accompaniment.
- **Taan:** Fast melodic runs.
