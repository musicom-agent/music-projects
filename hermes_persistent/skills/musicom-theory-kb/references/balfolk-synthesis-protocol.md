# Balfolk / Folk Synthesis Protocol

When synthesizing for the Balfolk genre, priority shift from "ethereal sustain" to "rhythmic bite".

## Rhythmic Drivers
- **Violin**: Use short attack (15ms) and bright harmonic roll-off (1/h^1.6) to cut through the mix. 
- **Guitar**: Use Karplus-Strong with faster decay (0.992) for a percussive 'comping' feel.
- **Meter**: Dancers require clear articulation on beats 1 and 4 in 6/8 (Jig) or beats 1 and 3 in 4/4 (Schottische).

## Pitch Calibration
- Absolute stability is preferred over 'natural jitter' for lead dance motifs to prevent frequency interference.
- Formants are used to provide 'wooden' character without sacrificing pitch-locking.
