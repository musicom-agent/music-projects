# Modal Interchange & Chromatic Resolution

## The "Bit-Shift" Theory
In the Musicom framework, modal interchange is treated as a logic gate operation on the 12-TET Pitch Class Matrix (bulbs 0-11).

## Gravitational Best Practices
- **Raised Notes (#4, #5, #1):** Creating "High Pressure". They must resolve UP to the nearest diatonic neighbor.
- **Lowered Notes (b6, b2, b3):** Creating "Low Pressure". They must resolve DOWN to the nearest diatonic neighbor.

## Integration Checklist
1. **Conservation of Identity:** Check for common tones. A jump with zero common tones is "Teleportation" (use sparingly).
2. **Visual Alignment:** Ensure the `chords_pc` list order in visualization scripts matches the `midi_notes` list in synthesis scripts to maintain the correct "Bass Gravity" representation.
3. **The Anchor:** Surround "Off-Scale" patterns with I or V chords from the Parent scale to prevent tonal drift.
