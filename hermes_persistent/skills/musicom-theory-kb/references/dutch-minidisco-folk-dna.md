# Dutch Minidisco-Folk DNA

## Genre Definition
A commercial hybrid of traditional Dutch children's folk (simple diatonic melodies, 4/4 marching pulse) and Euro-Minidisco (holiday park anthems with synthesized leads and driving kicks).

## Structural Patterns
- **Meter**: 4/4.
- **Tempo**: 100 BPM (Walking) to 120 BPM (Energy Dance).
- **Harmony**: Bb Major focus (I-IV-V-ii). Circle of fifths "bright" progressions.
- **Rhythm DNA**: `█░░░█░░░█░░░█░░░` (Four-on-the-floor) + Clap on beats 2 & 4.
- **Melodic Contour**: Arpeggiated hooks, upward striving, repetitive anthemic intervals (1-2-3-1 or 5-5-5-1).

## Instrumental Palette
- **Bass**: Thick sine or sub-bass, root-fifth walking patterns.
- **Lead**: Bright Sawtooth synths with ~5.5Hz vibrato.
- **Acoustic**: Strummed acoustic guitars and park-specific "Forest" or "Beach" soundscapes.

## Soundscapes (Numpy implementations)
- **Forest**: Sine frequency sweeps (2kHz-4kHz) for chirps; Low FM noise (50Hz) for bear growls.
- **Beach**: White noise swells with sine-wave amplitude modulation (period = 1 bar).
- **Rabbit Jump**: Staccato blip (800Hz) with fast exponential decay on the off-beats.
