### Musicom AI FinOps & Economic Optimization
The framework incorporates five primary economic levers to reduce blended costs (from $18.40 to $2.31/1M tokens):

- **Model Routing**: ~8x reduction by routing symbolic MIDI work to efficiency models.
- **Context Compaction**: 50-70% reduction in long-session token overhead for agentic music workflows.
- **Draft Cheap, Finalise Expensive**: Draft structures/sketches on efficient models; finalise audio-rich campaigns with precision models.
- **Prompt Caching**: Reduces cost to ~10% for repeatedly-loaded skills like this one.
- **AI FinOps Instrumentation**: Measurable prerequisite for scaling operations.

## Top AI Music Composition Tools

### Commercial / SaaS
- **Suno AI** — Text-to-full-song (vocals + music + lyrics)
- **Udio** — Similar to Suno, production-quality
- **AIVA** — Classical/cinematic/emotional composition, highly customizable
- **Staccato** — Professional DAW-integrated, ethical AI training, VST/MIDI output
- **Beatoven.ai** — Royalty-free background music for video
- **Soundraw** — Customizable AI music, no music training needed
- **Amper Music / Shutterstock** — Acquired, still referenced
- **Ecrett Music** — Scene-based quick generation
- **Lemonaide** — Beginner-friendly with guided features

### Open Source / Research
- **MusicGen** (Meta/AudioCraft) — text-to-music, music continuation, music reproduction
- **MusicLM** (Google) — hierarchical seq-to-seq model
- **Magenta** (Google/TensorFlow) — MusicVAE, Cyclegan, Music RNN, Studio plugins
- **Jukebox** (OpenAI) — high-quality + voice conditioning
- **Riffusion** — spectrogram diffusion models
- **MuseNet** (OpenAI) — 10 instruments, long-form generation
- **Flow Machines** (Sony) — AI composition research (ex-Bell Labs)

### Controlled Generation
- Text-to-music
- Melody continuation
- Style transfer
- Harmony→melody mapping

## Musical Skills Relevant to AI

- **Harmony**: diatonic, extended chords, modal interchange, chromatic mediants
- **Melody**: motivic development (sequence, inversion, retrograde, augmentation), phrase structure
- **Rhythm**: Euclidean, polyrhythms (3:2, 4:3), syncopation, swing, clave patterns
- **Form**: binary, ternary, rondo, sonata, theme & variations, verse-chorus
- **Orchestration**: ranges, doubling, articulation, dynamics, spectral layering
