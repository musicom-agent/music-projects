# Genre Pattern Matrix Education

Use this when the user asks to learn genres through pattern-based composition.

## Core Method

- Pattern = musical DNA: rhythm, pitch, harmony, bass, melody, timbre, form.
- Matrix = learning/composition body: rows are layers, columns are bars or sections.
- Teach each genre by extracting its patterns, filling the matrix, then composing an etude.

## Universal Genre Study Matrix

```text
Rows: Rhythm | Bass | Harmony | Melody | Timbre | Form
Cols: Bar 1 | Bar 2 | Bar 3 | Bar 4 | Bar 5 | Bar 6 | Bar 7 | Bar 8
```

For each row ask:

- Rhythm: pulses, gravity, swing/dotted/Euclidean logic?
- Bass: root, pedal, walking, ostinato?
- Harmony: modal loop, functional cadence, ii-V-I, pedal, static vamp?
- Melody: contour, motif, chord-tone targets, ornament, call-response?
- Timbre: acoustic, electronic, orchestral, hybrid?
- Form: AABB, 12-bar, AABA, loop blocks, narrative arc?

## Genre Capsules

### Balfolk
- Function: dance body.
- Meters: 6/8 jig, 3/4 mazurka, 4/4 schottische, bourree 2-beat drive.
- Rhythm DNA: jig `█░░█░░`; mazurka beat-2 lean; schottische dotted pointé.
- Pitch: Dorian/Aeolian/Mixolydian, mostly conjunct within a 10th.
- Harmony: modal loops: i-VII-i-IV, i-VII-IV-i, I-bVII-IV-I.
- Form: AABB, repeatable dance phrases.

### Jazz
- Function: harmonic motion and improvisation.
- Rhythm: 4/4 swing, backbeat 2 and 4, syncopated comping.
- Pitch: chord tones on strong beats, chromatic approaches/enclosures on weak beats.
- Harmony: ii-V-I, I-vi-ii-V, 7ths/9ths/13ths.
- Form: 12-bar blues, 32-bar AABA, head-solos-head.

### Classical
- Function: architecture and development.
- Rhythm: motor 8ths/16ths, sequence rhythm.
- Pitch: small motif transformed by sequence, inversion, augmentation.
- Harmony: I-IV-V-I, circle of fifths, cadences.
- Form: antecedent-consequent, binary/ternary/rondo/sonata.

### Electronic / Techno
- Function: loop plus timbral evolution.
- Rhythm DNA: kick `█░░░█░░░█░░░█░░░`; offbeat hats; syncopated bass.
- Pitch: short cells, Aeolian/Phrygian/minor pentatonic.
- Harmony: static loop, one-chord vamp, modal pedal.
- Form: 8/16/32-bar intro-build-drop-break blocks.

### Cinematic
- Function: narrative emotion.
- Rhythm: ostinato, spiccato strings, taiko/low drum pulses.
- Pitch: modal color. Lydian=wonder, Phrygian=danger, Aeolian=sorrow.
- Harmony: pedal point, modal interchange, chromatic mediants, neoriemannian shifts.
- Form: atmosphere -> tension -> peak -> release.

## Exercise Arc: Genre Matrix Etudes

Create five 8-bar studies, then hybrids.

1. Balfolk Dorian Jig
   - D Dorian, 6/8, rhythm `█░░█░░`, Dm-C-Dm-G.
   - Accent beats 1 and 4; end on D or A.
2. Jazz ii-V-I Swing
   - C major, 4/4 swing, Dm7-G7-Cmaj7.
   - Chord tones on strong beats, approach tones on weak beats.
3. Classical Motif Development
   - G major, 4/4, I-V-vi-IV-ii-V-I-I.
   - One 3-note motif, sequence, invert, cadence.
4. Electronic Techno Loop
   - A Aeolian, 4/4, 128 BPM, Am-Am-F-G.
   - Add one layer every 2 bars; interest via timbre/density.
5. Cinematic Phrygian Ostinato
   - E Phrygian, Em-F-Em-Dm-Em-F-C-Em.
   - Emphasize b2 (F) over E pedal for danger.
