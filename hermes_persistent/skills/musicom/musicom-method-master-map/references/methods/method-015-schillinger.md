# Method 015: Schillinger System of Musical Design

## Source
Joseph Schillinger (1941)

## Musical Elements Strategy
- **PITCH**: Derived from the "Geometrical Projection" of a melody axis. Intervals are treated as coordinate displacements.
- **RHYTHM**: Primary generative driver. Built from **Binary Resultants** ($r = a \div b$), the interference pattern between two periodicites.
- **HARMONY**: "Special Theory of Harmony" using algebraic root cycles (Cyclic $C$ or Tonal $T$) and permutations of specific voicings.
- **STRUCTURE**: Defined by the duration of the master resultant cycles.
- **TEXTURE**: Referred to as **Strata**. Defined by temporal density (events per time unit) and vertical/registral density.

## UnitMatrix Bridge
- **Resultant Mapping**: Assign specific generator pairs to rows (Voices). Phase-shift or invert resultants across columns (Sections) to create structural development.
- **Axis Projection**: Map geometrical coordinates directly into PITCH cells.
- **Strata Assignment**: Control Voice activity and register bands via Schillinger's density formulas.
